Process

How the forum works

01

Submit a position

Choose a debate topic and submit your argument. Keep it concise, evidence-based, and under 600 characters.

02

AI evaluation

A leader validator runs AI judgment on your argument, scoring it on reasoning quality, relevance, and evidence.

03

Consensus reached

Multiple validators re-run the evaluation. Agreement on the decision confirms the verdict on-chain.

04

Reputation builds

Your scores accumulate across debates. High-quality deliberation earns a verifiable on-chain reputation.

Architecture

No backend.
The contract
is the backend.

On GenLayer, there are no servers, no databases, no API keys. State, business logic, and AI judgment all live inside the Intelligent Contract under validator consensus. The frontend is a static SPA that talks directly to the chain.

Read the GenLayer docs
A

AI under consensus

The LLM judgment is re-run by multiple validators. Agreement on the decision confirms the verdict on-chain, not on one server.

B

Injection resistant

User text is capped and treated as untrusted data. The AI prompt enforces strict rules that nothing in user input can override.

C

Open deliberation

Anyone can submit arguments. The contract evaluates quality based on reasoning, evidence, and relevance. No gatekeeping.
